 About the role 
 An exciting opportunity has opened up in Commercial Team / Davy Private Clients. In the role, the successful candidate will:
 
 Reporting & Performance Analysis 
 Produce and maintain regular management reports and dashboards.
 Monitor and analyse key business metrics, trends and performance indicators.
 Identify opportunities, risks and emerging trends through data analysis.
 Support the delivery of business performance reporting to management and key stakeholders.
 Develop reporting solutions that improve visibility, consistency and decision-making.
 Business Analysis & Decision Support 
 Conduct forecasting, trend analysis and scenario modelling to support business planning.
 Analyse business performance and provide actionable insights and recommendations.
 Assist management in evaluating business opportunities and areas for improvement.
 Support budgeting, forecasting and planning activities through analytical input.
 Translate data into meaningful management information and business intelligence.
 Performance Management Support 
 Support the production and ongoing development of performance scorecards and reporting frameworks.
 Assist in tracking performance against agreed objectives and key metrics.
 Provide analytical support to managers and business stakeholders.
 Ensure performance data is accurate, timely and actionable.
 Contribute to the continuous improvement of performance measurement processes.
 Data Management & Governance 
 Support data quality improvement initiatives across business systems and processes.
 Assist in maintaining data integrity, consistency and governance standards.
 Identify data issues and work with stakeholders to implement corrective actions.
 Contribute to the development and enhancement of reporting and data management processes.
 Support business readiness activities associated with system and technology initiatives.
 Stakeholder Engagement 
 Build strong working relationships across business functions.
 Act as a key liaison between operational teams and support functions in relation to reporting and analysis requirements.
 Gather and document business requirements for reporting and analytical needs.
 Present findings, insights and recommendations in a clear and concise manner.
 Support projects and initiatives that require analytical expertise and business insight.
 Requirements 
 About you 
 We are looking for somebody who has the following background and skills:
 Essential 
 Third-level qualification in Business, Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Data Analytics, Information Systems or a related discipline.
 Previous experience in a Business Analyst, Sales Operations Analyst, Reporting Analyst or similar analytical role.
 Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to interpret complex data sets.
 Experience producing management information, performance reporting and business insights.
 Advanced Microsoft Excel skills.
 Strong attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy.
 Ability to communicate analytical findings clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
 Strong organisational skills and ability to manage multiple priorities.
 Experience working with stakeholders across different business areas.
 Desirable 
 Experience with CRM systems such as Salesforce.
 Experience using business intelligence and reporting tools (e.g. Power BI, Tableau or similar).
 Experience supporting forecasting, planning or budgeting activities.
 Knowledge of data governance and data quality management practices.
 Experience working within financial services or a regulated industry.
 Relevant professional qualifications or industry certifications would be an advantage.
